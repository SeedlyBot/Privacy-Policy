# Privacy Policy for Seedly Bot

This Privacy Policy explains what information Seedly Bot collects and how it is used.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

We may collect and store the following information:

- Discord user ID
- Discord username and/or display name
- Discord server ID, if needed for server-related settings
- Game-related data such as farm progress, inventory, items, currency, cooldowns, stats, and leaderboard data

If you use Supporter features through Patreon, we may also process limited Patreon-related information needed to verify your Supporter status, such as:

- Patreon membership status
- Patreon tier or benefit eligibility
- linked account identifiers, if needed to connect Patreon benefits to your Discord account

We do not store message content.

We do not intentionally collect more information than necessary to operate the Bot.

## How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

- provide and maintain the Bot's features
- store your game progress and inventory
- run leaderboards and game systems
- verify and provide Supporter benefits
- prevent abuse, cheating, and exploits
- improve the stability and functionality of the Service

Some information, such as usernames, display names, and leaderboard data, may be visible to other users through the Bot's features.

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not share your data with third parties except when necessary to operate the Service (for example, hosting or database providers), when needed to verify Patreon-based Supporter benefits, or when required by law.

## Patreon and Payments

Supporter subscriptions are processed by Patreon.

We do not receive or store your full payment details, such as full card information. Payment processing, billing, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Patreon under Patreon's own terms and policies.
